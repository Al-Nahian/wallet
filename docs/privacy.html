Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what data the Wallet Android app ("Wallet", "the app") collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. Wallet is built to be local-first: using the app does not require an account, and most people will never have any data leave their device.

Data stored locally on your device

All the financial data you enter — accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring payments, savings goals, labels, and templates — is stored in a local database on your device. This data is never transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly choose to sign in (see below).

SMS access

If you grant the SMS permission, Wallet reads incoming SMS messages on-device to automatically detect and pre-fill transactions from bank and mobile-wallet alerts (e.g. bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank debit/credit notifications). This parsing happens entirely on your device:

  • Raw SMS content is never stored permanently and never leaves your device.
  • Only the extracted transaction summary (amount, merchant/payee, date) is kept, and only after you review and confirm it.
  • This permission is optional — you can use Wallet fully by entering transactions manually if you prefer not to grant it.

Notifications

The notifications permission is used only for the app's own in-app alerts — budget warnings, upcoming recurring payments, goal milestones — and is never used to deliver advertising or third-party content.

Optional account sign-in and cloud sync

Signing in is entirely optional and is never required to use the app. If you choose to sign in with Google, Microsoft, or an email/password, Wallet uses Supabase as its authentication and backend provider. Signing in:

  • Stores a basic profile (display name, email address, avatar if provided by Google/Microsoft) tied to your account, used only to identify your account within Wallet.
  • Optionally backs up your financial data to encrypted cloud storage so you can restore it or sync it across your own devices.
  • Your data is protected by database-level access rules (Row Level Security) so that only your own signed-in account can ever read or write your data — not other users, and not us.

Signing out does not delete your local data. You can request deletion of your cloud account and all associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • We never sell or share your financial data with third parties.
  • We don't read your SMS inbox for anything other than on-device transaction parsing.

Children's privacy

Wallet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy
