🪙 Wallet

A local-first personal finance tracker for Android.

Wallet helps you track accounts, transactions, budgets, recurring payments, and savings goals — with strong support for Bangladesh-specific accounts like bKash, Nagad, and Rocket alongside regular bank and cash accounts.

  • Works fully offline — your data lives on your device by default.
  • Optional sign-in (Google, Microsoft, or email) enables encrypted cloud backup and syncing across your own devices.
  • Automatic transaction capture from bank/mobile-wallet SMS alerts, processed entirely on-device.
  • Budgets, recurring transactions, savings goals, and spending reports.
